Academic Profile

Dr Valentine Joseph Owan is a Nigerian researcher, educator, and Lecturer in the Department of Educational Psychology at the University of Calabar, Nigeria. He specialises in Research, Measurement and Evaluation, with core research specialisations in Research Evaluation and Scientometrics. His academic interests centre on understanding how evidence can be used to assess research performance, explain variations in research outcomes, and support the improvement of research practice.

He conducts studies on research productivity, publication behaviour, academic capacity, bibliometric indicators, university ranking, and the application of artificial intelligence in tackling research problems in the field of education and health. These studies are intended to generate evidence for improving research quality, informing policy and practice, and strengthening research systems in higher education.

Research Themes

The principal areas of his research and scholarly contributions.

Research Evaluation

Research evaluation refers to the systematic assessment of research performance using appropriate evidence and measures. It provides a basis for assessing the quality, productivity, influence, and development of research at individual, institutional, and national levels. He conducts studies in this area on research productivity, academic capacity, publication behaviour, research quality, and evidence based approaches for strengthening research systems.

Scientometrics

Scientometrics refers to the quantitative study of scientific research and scholarly communication using bibliometric methods and related measures. It is used to assess patterns of publication, citation, collaboration, research output, and research visibility. He applies these methods to study scientific research, university performance, research impact, and indicators used for research assessment and institutional ranking.

Scholarly Communication

Scholarly communication refers to the processes through which research findings are prepared, reviewed, published, disseminated, and used within the academic community. It covers the activities involved in communicating research through journals and other scholarly outlets, including publication practices, peer review, authorship, responsible publishing, and research integrity. He conducts studies on publication persistence, journal selection, peer review, authorship practices, responsible publishing, research integrity, and strategies for improving research visibility.

Educational Measurement and Psychometrics

Educational measurement refers to the systematic process of assigning numerical values to educational and psychological characteristics using appropriate measurement procedures. Psychometrics is concerned with the development and evaluation of instruments used to measure such characteristics. He develops and validates educational and psychological instruments, assesses constructs, estimates reliability, evaluates validity, and applies psychometric principles to educational research and programme evaluation.

Research Analytics

Research analytics refers to the use of quantitative methods and analytical procedures to examine research data and support evidence based decision making. He applies structural equation modelling, bibliometric analysis, machine learning, artificial neural networks, and other multivariate techniques to analyse research data, develop predictive models, and assess research performance.

Higher Education Research

Higher education refers to formal education provided beyond secondary school through institutions such as universities, polytechnics, and colleges. It includes teaching, learning, research, assessment, academic development, and the administration of educational institutions. His research in this area is mainly concerned with research and related issues within higher education, with particular attention to academic staff, postgraduate students, universities, research capacity, institutional performance, university governance, educational policy, and factors associated with research productivity and scholarly development.

Research Philosophy and Vision

The principles that guide his research and the direction of his long-term scholarly programme.

He believes that good research should generate reliable evidence for understanding problems, making informed decisions, improving institutions, and addressing practical needs. He therefore gives attention to sound measurement, methodological rigour, transparent reporting, responsible scholarly communication, and the proper use of evidence in research.

He seeks to advance research evaluation and scientometrics through approaches that can be used to measure, explain, predict, and improve research performance. He combines educational measurement, bibliometric analysis, artificial intelligence, and quantitative methods to address questions in higher education and research systems, with particular interest in research productivity, scholarly communication, research capacity, and the use of evidence to improve research practice.
