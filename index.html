Research, Measurement and Evaluation

Dr. Valentine Joseph Owan

This website showcases the academic work of Dr Valentine Joseph Owan, including research, publications, teaching, editorial service, and professional activities. His research focuses on understanding how research performance can be measured, explained, predicted, and improved through research evaluation, scientometrics, scholarly communication, educational measurement, and research analytics.

Research Themes

The major themes that define the current research programme.

Research Evaluation

Examining how research quality, productivity, performance, and impact can be assessed to support evidence-based decision making within universities and research systems.

Scientometrics

Studying publication output, citation patterns, collaboration networks, bibliometric indicators, and university research performance through quantitative approaches.

Scholarly Communication

Investigating publication behaviour, peer review, research dissemination, research integrity, authorship, and strategies that strengthen scholarly publishing.

Educational Measurement

Developing and evaluating instruments, measurement models, and assessment practices that improve educational and behavioural research.

Research Analytics

Applying structural equation modelling, bibliometric analysis, machine learning, psychometrics, and multivariate statistics to answer complex research questions.

Higher Education Research

Examining research capacity, academic development, institutional culture, collaboration, and policies that influence research performance within universities.

Current Projects

Ongoing studies contributing to research evaluation, scientometrics, and higher education research.

Research Evaluation and University Performance

Current work examines academic capacity, publication behaviour, institutional support, collaboration, and research productivity among university academics. The overall aim is to develop evidence that strengthens research systems and supports better institutional decision making.

Scientometric Studies

Ongoing studies examine publication trends, citation impact, collaboration networks, bibliometric indicators, and approaches for evaluating university research performance using quantitative and computational methods.

Artificial Intelligence in Research

Current projects investigate the responsible application of artificial intelligence to research design, academic writing, educational measurement, scholarly communication, and higher education.

Instrument Development and Validation

Continuing work focuses on the development, validation, and refinement of educational and psychological instruments for research, assessment, and programme evaluation.
